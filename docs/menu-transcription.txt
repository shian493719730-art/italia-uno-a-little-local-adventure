ITALIA UNO — PHOTO MENU TRANSCRIPTION
Prepared 7 November 2026 for the independent Creative Track prototype.

SCOPE
All readable names and explicitly named variants in the three blackboards of the supplied close photograph are included. The wider photograph was used as a second visual cross-check. This is NOT a claim that the photographs show the restaurant's entire current menu. Additional unseen boards, bottled drinks, unlisted sweets and live stock cannot be inferred.

METHOD
1. Visually inspected both full supplied photographs.
2. Created temporary enlarged crops of each blackboard solely to read the source. No crop is used as game art, and the original photos were not modified.
3. Transcribed original names and readable fillings.
4. Expanded explicit named OR choices, named hot-drink sizes/double-shot variants, and three named glass-gelato flavours into separate searchable entries. The four panino add-ons stay with their panino rather than being counted as separate dishes; Cotoletta adds cheese, Piccante adds sun-dried tomatoes, Bondola adds artichoke hearts, Veneziano adds aubergines. (Four add-on rows total.)
5. English descriptions use readable board wording. Chinese descriptions are translations, not independently verified recipes or allergen declarations.
6. The Italian and English fresh-orange-juice lines are one drink and are not double-counted.
7. Historic prices are omitted. The two photos show different prices; some digits are covered by food packaging. No game score or serving fee is represented as a restaurant price.

LIMITS / OCCLUDED DETAILS
- Prices on the right board have covered digits; none are guessed or published.
- The two generic lines “Gelati” and “Coppe” are retained. Their exact relationship to the following named-flavour glass row is unspecified. Both entries carry an explicit format-unspecified note.
- Pasta of the day does not name a particular daily pasta recipe. The vegetarian/meat choices remain one catalogue entry with an explicit note.
- The board says “Dolci (sweets) — ask staff what's available” and gives examples ending “etc.” No extra unnamed dessert has been invented.
- Individual fillings for insalata vegetariana and antipasto grande are not legible/listed; those are not filled in by assumption.
- “Torta nonna” is kept as written, rather than silently substituting a presumed full recipe name.
- The board spells “Camomile”; that spelling is preserved.
- Nastrine is readable in the enlarged close photo; it is not “d'astrice”.
- There is no legible separate bottled-cold-drink, alcohol or full allergen list in the supplied boards. Only freshly squeezed orange juice is confirmed here.
